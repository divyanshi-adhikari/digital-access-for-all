Offline emergency guidance & essential contacts

🚨 Emergency SOPs

🌍 Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On
  • Stay away from windows
  • Do not use elevators

🌊 Flood

  • Move to higher ground immediately
  • Avoid walking through moving water
  • Disconnect electrical appliances

🔥 Fire

  • Use stairs, not elevators
  • Crawl low under smoke
  • Stop, Drop, and Roll if clothes catch fire

🌪️ Tsunami / Tornado

  • Move inland or to higher ground
  • Follow official evacuation routes
  • Stay away from coastal areas

☀️ Heatwave

  • Drink water frequently
  • Avoid outdoor activity during peak heat
  • Wear light-colored clothing

❄️ Coldwave

  • Wear layered warm clothes
  • Avoid prolonged exposure
  • Keep indoor heating safe

🩺 Health & First Aid

🩸 Bleeding Control

  • Apply firm direct pressure
  • Raise injured area if possible
  • Use clean cloth or bandage

❤️ CPR

  • Check responsiveness
  • Call emergency services
  • 30 compressions : 2 breaths

🐍 Snake / Insect Bite

  • Keep victim calm and still
  • Do NOT cut or suck wound
  • Seek medical help immediately
